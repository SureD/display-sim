# Research history: M1–M18

Research starts with a physical question, distinguishes mechanism evidence from
sample measurements, records missing conditions, and hands an explicit model and
acceptance criteria to implementation. Notes retain their historical proposals
and source-access limitations; the [architecture](../architecture.md) and
[profile contracts](../profiles.md) describe current behavior. No new source
verification was performed during M16's repository reorganization.

M0 established the PM plan. M1–M6 built the research and validation basis; later
milestones used it for opt-in experiments with default-output parity. The
chronology below follows milestone numbers. M8 provenance landed before the M7
implementation, and M12a/M12b were parallel workstreams; git history and the
[PM status record](../reports/STATUS.md) preserve their integration order.

| **M9: replay and drive fitting** | Can named window evidence inform an experiment without double limiting or changing generic presets? | Scalar targets identify only a proposed per-window drive, and shipped records lack matched amplitude/timing. Low residual does not calibrate arbitrary content. | Add explicit strict/exploratory matching and separate exclusive `.fit.json` datasets; each forward trial uses the original limiter once. See [M6 handoff](m6-profile-wiring.md#8-implementation-handoff-and-remaining-nr-data) and [M9 contract](../profiles.md#m9-window-replay-and-drive-fitting). |
| **M10: viewing angle** | Can white transmission, black leakage, gray wash-out, and OLED emission be varied independently? | Sparse older angular anchors cannot fit the current generic presets; IPS leakage and OLED gain/spectra remain NR. | Add a default-off center-ray 0–45° P model and angular snapshots; retain exact on-axis parity. [M5 mechanism](m5-fidelity.md#2-viewing-angle-leakage-white-gain-and-gamma-need-separate-fits), [M6 source boundaries](m6-profile-wiring.md#3-angular-leakage-separate-va-and-ips-fits), and [M10 schema](../profiles.md#angular-schema-version-1-m10) document the handoff. |
| **M11: OLED dynamics design** | What capacity state, clock, initialization, and acceptance criteria can support attack/release? | Static windows do not identify dynamics constants; one global nit-capacity owner, full prior history, and explicit e-fold times are needed. | Design a separate history-aware opt-in experiment, reject ambiguous legacy/M9 compositions, and retain measured constants NR. [Design research](m11-oled-dynamics.md) becomes the M13 implementation contract. |
| **M12a: browser HDR/EDR preview** | Can Chrome present linear output with honest capability and calibration controls? | Extended WebGPU is a presentation route; capability flags do not establish headroom or emitted nits. The tested software path could not validate HDR light output. | Export linear-nit assets and use one shared brightness transform, meter-based white reference, and SDR fallbacks. See [Chrome research](m12a-web-hdr.md) and [viewer protocol](../../web/README.md). |
| **M12b: principles explainer** | How can users inspect the pipeline and core mechanisms without reading Python? | Interactive diagrams need one numerical model per instrument, explicit P/NR limits, and source checks against current code. | Build an offline eight-chapter explainer with local-dimming, PQ/ABL, and pursuit instruments. [Sources](../../web-explainer/SOURCES.md) and [verification](../../web-explainer/VERIFICATION.md) record model/browser checks. |
| **M13: OLED dynamics runtime** | Can the M11 design advance one capacity history per tick and resume exactly? | Capacity can share the existing shoulder when timing/identity/history are explicit; partial initialization is insufficient. No matched controller calibration is available. | Implement default-off capacity attack/release, full checkpoints, synthetic protocol, and separate reports. See [M11 acceptance criteria](m11-oled-dynamics.md#acceptance-tests-and-parity-gate) and [M13 schema](../profiles.md#oled-dynamics-protocol-schema-version-1-m13). |
| **M14: block-motion proxy** | Can deterministic estimated motion expose interpolation artifacts beyond known translation? | Reduced-raster matching needs confidence, visibility, cuts, and fallback reasons; occlusion, thin text, repeated texture, and large motion remain difficult. | Add default-off `mci`, stream adjacent pairs, preserve one display update/tick, and validate seven synthetic truth fixtures. [Motion research](m7-memc-motion.md) motivates the proxy; [artifact report](../../reports/motion-m14/README.md) records observed errors and limitations. |
| **M15: colorimetry and volume** | How should source/panel primaries, gamut mapping, WOLED color ceilings, and exports be explicit? | Container primaries differ from transfer/mastering declarations; exact matrices are D under their inputs. Straight u′v′ hue rays are not perceptual constant hue, and panel spectra/color-volume calibration remain NR. | Add default-off declared colorimetry, strict P panel sidecars, clip/compression and one joint color/HDR capacity owner. Preserve signed export coordinates and legacy parity. See [primary-source research](m15-colorimetry.md), [schema](../profiles.md#colorimetry-schema-version-1-m15), and [saved comparison](../../reports/colorimetry-m15/README.md). |
| **M17: LCD zone dynamics** | How can local-dimming lag, halo trails and smoothing trade-offs be modeled without changing default rendering? | Patents and IEEE work support filtering zone commands before optical reconstruction and LCD compensation; reviewer transition tests support the artifact mechanisms but supply no calibrated zone tau. | Define an opt-in P asymmetric per-zone controller, explicit clocks/cuts/initialization/full-history continuation, strict state ownership and matched off/on synthetic metrics. See [research and implementation handoff](m17-zone-temporal.md). |
| **M18: sub-frame emission** | How can LC response, OD, PWM/strobe/BFI, pursuit blur and flicker be exposed within a display tick? | Primary LCD/SID/IEEE work supports separate response, emission and pursuit domains; original measurement examples depend on specimen/settings. Time-only blur and simple flicker metrics cannot establish a quality or health rating. | Define an opt-in P model with rational emission timing, one owner per history, explicit composition/rejection and exact continuation; measured waveform/LUT calibration remains NR. See [research and implementation handoff](m18-subframe-temporal.md). |

## Reading evidence and historical paths

A research source can establish a mechanism without establishing its numeric
settings for a generic panel. Each later schema retains the distinction between
`M` measurements, `D` derivations, `P` proposed choices, and `NR` unknowns. Notes
also preserve unsuccessful source retrieval and conditions not reported by the
publisher. Follow the original source locators before making new accuracy claims.

All research Markdown moved here in M16; its original technical substance is
retained. Versioned synthetic JSON protocols/configs now ship under
[`src/tv_sim/data/measurements/`](../../src/tv_sim/data/measurements/).
Historical `research/`/`sim/` locators embedded in unchanged data use the
[path aliases](../profiles.md#historical-paths-and-packaged-data).
