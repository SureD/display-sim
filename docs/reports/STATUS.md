## Drift / risks
- Profile numbers remain placeholders; do not present as emulating a specific TV.
- M15 is default off. Its panel xy/white, radial compression knee and WOLED RGBW envelope are P; matrices and reported errors are D under those inputs. Hue preservation means a straight CIE u′v′ ray, not uniform perceived hue. Full pipeline optics/transmission/angular changes can alter chromaticity after mapping. One joint capacity combines the color envelope with static ABL/M13; nonzero legacy power/backlight-tracking limiters and ambiguous legacy matrices/angles reject. Spectra, measured primaries/color-volume and RGBW electrical fits remain NR; signed linear exports do not establish host gamut or emitted nits. SDR sheets can hide wide-gamut differences.
- MEMC defaults off; exact captured arrays/assets/traces and histories protect the off path and phase-1 repeat/analytic modes. M14 remains a P block proxy: ambiguous/repeated texture and uncertain boundaries can cause substantial holds or edge artifacts. Large motion outside search support is not reliably measurable; confidence can force a safe hold without identifying its true magnitude. The simple cut detector can miss similar shots or mistake appearance changes; declared cuts are authoritative. Lookahead is an assumed adjacent source interval, separate from offline compute time and measured TV latency (NR).
- M10 uses P synthetic endpoints over a center-ray angle of 0–45°, with unsigned symmetry and azimuth independence. It does not model per-pixel viewing geometry or IPS corner glow. Angular panel terms precede the existing HDR/ABL/temporal stages, so emitted-load ABL can interact with angular gains; the gallery isolates the terms with fixed-backlight SDR and disabled load limiting. No named TV is calibrated.
- M9 is opt-in with separate fitted dataset IDs. Shipped records have `NR` amplitude/timing, so exploratory assumptions are required; low fitting residuals do not establish a calibrated TV, sustained capability or arbitrary-content replay. Strict geometry rejects raster area mismatch; exploratory rounding remains `P`.
- M13 implements the M11 synthetic controller; its target map, policies and attack/release constants remain `P`. Measured dynamics, timed sustained traces, thermal/static-image controllers, RGBW allocation and watt coefficients remain `NR`. Updated endpoint capacity is held for the tick exposure (explicit approximation). M9 v1 drives cannot migrate automatically; measured dynamics fitting needs a matched-history dataset.
