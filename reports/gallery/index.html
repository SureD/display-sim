Four TVs, one source.

Placeholder parameters, not measurements or retail-TV emulations. HDR is scaled into an SDR sRGB preview. Your screen cannot reproduce the simulated peak or black level. Every comparison row uses a shared exposure; there is no per-TV auto-normalization.

Click a contact sheet for its full-size PNG. This folder works offline with relative image paths. The default columns are VA mini-LED, IPS edge LCD, WOLED, QD-OLED. All receive identical float PQ sources; ambient is 0 lux, on-axis, and profile spatial processing stays enabled.

Protocol, scales and profile settings

HDR rows: 2,000 simulated nits maps to preview white. Shadow diagnostics: 10 nits maps to white (200× exposure); brighter pixels clip. Motion: 100 nits maps to white. Macro apertures: 1,000 maps to white. These are exposure choices, not a calibrated observer model or HDR encoding.

Motion uses 12 settled pre-roll frames plus 36 advancing pan frames at approximately 60 Hz, 8 pixels/frame (~480 pixels/s), 16 subframe integration samples. Each mode starts with fresh state. The final-frame snapshots model pursuit integration, not a stationary-camera photograph. OD is an explicit LCD-only probe (gain 0.8, 4-ms prefix); OLED columns repeat full hold. BFI is an assumed centered 25%-duty single pulse at fixed peak, without brightness compensation. Neither probe claims a supported retail mode. No MEMC, scanout, PWM or measured response LUT is modeled.

Macro masks have a black matrix, equal logical-pixel pitch and arbitrary aperture dimensions. RGBW allocation and staggered triangular QD geometry are synthetic, not micrographs or a measured Pentile resolution model. Aperture amplitudes are area-normalized, so their peaks are not measured emitter nits. QD-OLED has a placeholder 0.45-pixel optical PSF; WOLED has none. The two OLED previews can therefore differ in edge softness.
